Led the design and delivery of an intensive summer course in probabilistic computing at UC Santa Barbara.

The course introduced students to Monte Carlo sampling, Metropolis-Hastings, Gibbs sampling, Ising and Potts models, simulated annealing, discrete optimization, probabilistic machine learning, and stochastic neural networks.

The course materials include lecture notes and executable notebooks for the main algorithms and demos used in the course:
