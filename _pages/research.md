## Research Overview

My research focuses on **probabilistic optimization in complex energy landscapes**, where traditional methods struggle due to ruggedness and exponential state spaces.

---

## Probabilistic Approximate Optimization Algorithm (PAOA)

PAOA is a quantum-inspired framework that learns adaptive sampling strategies to accelerate convergence in Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC).

### Key Ideas

* Learn annealing schedules instead of fixing them
* Bias proposal distributions using problem structure
* Optimize sampling dynamics, not just energy

---

## Why it matters

Many NP-hard problems (e.g., k-SAT, spin glasses) require exploring exponentially large spaces.

PAOA improves:

* convergence speed
* success probability
* scalability

---

## Current Directions

* Learning proposal distributions (make/break dynamics)
* Scaling behavior on k-SAT
* Hardware implementations (FPGA, p-bits)
